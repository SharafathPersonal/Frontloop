# FrontLoop

> FrontLoop is an autonomous B2B WhatsApp AI lead and business agent for small and medium businesses such as clinics, coaching institutes, salons and local service providers. It answers customer questions 24/7 on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, captures leads into a CRM dashboard, and hands conversations to human staff when needed.

FrontLoop acts as an always-on AI front desk. Each business configures its own knowledge base (services, prices, timings, operating hours and FAQs), and the AI answers only from that verified information. Customer name, phone number, intent and requirement are captured into a lead pipeline that staff can filter, search and export to CSV. Staff can pause the bot on any conversation with one click (human takeover).
